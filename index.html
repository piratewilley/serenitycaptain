FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you step aboard.

How many guests can join?

Serenity charters are designed for private groups of up to six guests.

Is payment taken through this website?

No. The form sends a booking request. We personally confirm availability and provide the next steps.

Can we choose our destinations?

Yes. We shape the itinerary around your interests while considering trip length, weather, sea conditions, and safety.

What happens if the weather is poor?

We will communicate directly and discuss the safest and best available option.

Can we call or text instead of using the form?

Absolutely. Call or text (340) 201-2285, or email book@serenitycaptain.com.
